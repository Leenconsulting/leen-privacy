1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Leen collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information when people use the Leen platform, website and related services. It also explains how Leen handles uploaded professional material that may contain commercially sensitive or personal information.

Leen is operated by Leen Teams Pty Ltd (ABN 18 689 585 760). In this policy, “Leen”, “we” and “us” mean Leen Teams Pty Ltd.

2Information we collect

  • Registration of interest: name, email address, company, job title, location and any other details you choose to give when you register interest in Leen or sign up for a Leen event or campaign.
  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number, password credentials, business name, role, where you are based, the country where your business is registered and its business number (such as an ABN), billing details and account preferences.
  • Profile information: consultant biography, skills, experience, location, industry focus, rates, availability, work history, insurances you choose to list and profile content.
  • Uploaded professional material: case studies, proposals, frameworks, credentials, CVs, documents, transcripts, voice notes, prompts, outputs and other materials uploaded or generated through Leen.
  • Workspace information: firm name, team members, permissions, shared libraries, usage activity and administrator settings.
  • Payment information: subscription plan, invoices, billing address, payment status and limited payment metadata. Card details are processed by our payment provider and are not stored directly by Leen unless expressly stated.
  • Usage and technical information: device, browser, IP address, log data, pages visited, feature usage, AI actions, error reports and analytics data.
  • Member activity: connections, shortlists, chats and messages with other members, reach-outs, blocks and team activity on the platform.
  • Communications: support requests, survey responses, feedback, emails and other communications with Leen.
  • Clients and other people who are not members: when a member shares a proposal or recommendation with you, or asks you to verify work, we collect your name, organisation, email address and any note you add. Verified details appear on the member’s profile unless you choose to stay anonymous.
  • People invited to Leen: when someone invites you to Leen, we collect your name and email address, the inviter’s email address and whether they say you have worked together, so we can send the invitation and keep track of referrals.

3How we collect information

  • directly from users when they create accounts, upload content, complete profiles, use features or contact support;
  • from forms you complete when you register interest in Leen, on our website or on social media platforms such as LinkedIn;
  • from people who invite you to Leen, who give us your name and email address;
  • automatically through the platform, cookies, analytics and logging tools;
  • from payment, authentication, analytics, support and other service providers; and
  • from authorised workspace administrators or team members.

4How we use information

  • provide, operate, maintain and improve the Leen platform;
  • create profiles, case studies, proposals and other user-requested outputs;
  • process subscriptions, billing, invoices and account administration;
  • authenticate users and manage workspace permissions;
  • deliver AI-assisted features requested by users;
  • provide customer support and troubleshoot issues;
  • protect the security and integrity of the platform;
  • analyse usage, adoption, feature performance and product quality;
  • create de-identified, aggregated insights about what helps members win work (see section 6);
  • send service, billing, security and product communications;
  • send alerts you have chosen to receive, by email, SMS or a messaging app;
  • send marketing communications where permitted, with opt-out options;
  • send invitations on behalf of people who invite you, and run referral programs and competitions;
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting, regulatory and dispute obligations.

5Uploaded professional material

Users may upload commercially sensitive professional material. Leen uses uploaded professional material to provide product features such as profile development, case study creation, proposal drafting, document search, capability matching and user-requested outputs.

Leen does not publish uploaded professional material, make it available to other users, sell it or use it to train shared AI models by default. Uploaded professional material is private unless the user chooses to share it or publish it through a platform feature. Source Documents, the files users upload for Leen to draw on, are never used for aggregated insights and are deleted automatically 12 months after last use unless the user chooses to keep them.

6De-identified, aggregated insights

Leen analyses structured features of content created in Leen, such as proposal structure, capabilities covered, team shape, pricing position and outcome, to produce aggregated insights, such as what winning proposals have in common. Each insight combines data from at least 20 members and is de-identified, so that no individual, firm or client can reasonably be identified. Leen does not use the text users write, their Source Documents, client names or pricing details for these insights.

De-identified information is not personal information. Leen may keep aggregated insights after an account is closed. Members can exclude their content from future insights in Settings.

7AI processing

Where users use AI-assisted features, Leen may process prompts, files, profile data, retrieved content and outputs through AI providers and related infrastructure providers. Leen uses these providers to deliver requested features and applies contractual or technical controls designed to protect user content.

8When we disclose information

  • to service providers that help us operate Leen, including hosting, storage, AI, payments, analytics, customer relationship management, email, messaging, security and support providers;
  • to authorised users within the same workspace or where a user chooses to share content;
  • to partner services you choose to connect to your Leen account, at your direction;
  • to professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants, auditors and insurers;
  • to regulators, law enforcement, courts or other parties where required or permitted by law;
  • in connection with a business transaction such as investment, merger, acquisition, restructure or sale, subject to confidentiality protections where appropriate;
  • to protect rights, safety, security, users, Leen or third parties.

9Overseas storage and processing

Leen may store or process information in Australia and in other countries where Leen or its service providers operate. Where required, Leen will take reasonable steps to protect information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection obligations.

10Security

Leen uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information and uploaded professional material from unauthorised access, misuse, interference, loss, modification or disclosure. No system is completely secure, and users should avoid uploading material they are not authorised to process through Leen.

11Retention and deletion

Leen retains information for as long as needed to provide the service, manage accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, prevent fraud and enforce agreements. Users may request deletion or export of their content. Deleted content may remain in backups for a limited period and may be retained where legally or operationally necessary. Source Documents are deleted automatically 12 months after last use unless the user chooses to keep them.

12Access, correction and deletion requests

Users may request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of their personal information by contacting linc@leen.consulting. Leen may need to verify identity before actioning a request. Some requests may be limited by legal, security, billing, dispute or technical requirements.

13Cookies and analytics

Leen may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, improve performance, understand usage and support marketing. Users may control cookies through browser settings, although disabling cookies may affect functionality.

14Marketing communications

Leen may send product updates, founder notes, research, event invitations and marketing communications where permitted. Users can unsubscribe from marketing communications, but may still receive transactional, billing, account and security messages.

15Complaints

Privacy complaints can be sent to linc@leen.consulting. Leen will respond within a reasonable period. Users may also have rights to complain to a privacy regulator, including the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in Australia or the Information Commissioner’s Office in the United Kingdom, depending on location and applicable law.

16Changes to this policy

Leen may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date. Material changes may be notified by email or in-product notice.
